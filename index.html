Project Portfolio Showcase

Redesign of PExA Instrument

Product Development Medical Tech

Helped design a medical instrument for identifying respiratory diseases. The project focused on improving ergonomics, usability, and clinical aesthetics. We built a functional physical prototype using CAD, 3D printing, and Arduino to evaluate and verify different design concepts. This involved optimizing the internal volume and creating intuitive user guidance.

B.Sc. Thesis: Sensorized Exoskeleton

Robotics Unity

Developed a lightweight, 3D printed sensorized exoskeleton aimed at gamifying the rehabilitation process for knee injuries. The system collects real time movement data using sensors. This data is processed to control a game application in Unity, motivating patients and ensuring they follow their exercises.

Modular Coffee Machine

Machine Design LCA & FMEA

Designed a sustainable and modular concept for a coffee machine, developing two different products from a shared architecture. The project utilized Failure Mode and Effects Analysis and Life Cycle Assessment to optimize production costs, manufacturability, and environmental impact.

Additive Manufacturing: Steering Knuckle

Additive Manufacturing Topology Optimization

Designed a lightweight and robust steering knuckle for a miniature terrain buggy using additive manufacturing and steel. Through topology optimization and structural simulation, the final part achieved significant weight reduction while maintaining necessary stiffness and strength for off road performance.
